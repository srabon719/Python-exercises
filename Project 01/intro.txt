              DHAKA CITY ECO CLEAN-UP MISSION
============================================================
Welcome to Dhaka! Our historic megacity faces serious challenges 
with litter, waste accumulation, and diminishing green cover. 

As a community volunteer, your mission is to explore key 
neighborhoods, restore dirty landmarks, and collect recyclable 
items for proper disposal. Every piece of trash collected 
brings us closer to a cleaner, greener Dhaka!
