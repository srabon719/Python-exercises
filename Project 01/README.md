# Clean Dhaka Adventure

A command-line text adventure game written in Python. The player acts as a volunteer in Dhaka to clean up pollution and improve the urban environment.

---

## 1. Game Idea & Objective

The game takes place in Dhaka across four main areas: Ramna Park, Buriganga River, Mirpur Street, and the Botanical Garden. 

Your goal is to help restore the city's environment. You can finish the game in one of two ways:
* **Route A (Collector Route):** Pick up at least 3 pieces of litter from around the city.
* **Route B (Action Route):** Use the `clean` command in at least 3 locations.

Completing either route wins the game!

---

## 2. Sustainable Development Goal (SDG)

This project connects to **SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities** and **SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production**:
* **SDG 11:** The game focuses on keeping city spaces clean, protecting local green areas, and improving river health.
* **SDG 12:** The player collects discarded items like plastic bottles, nets, and aluminum cans, showing the importance of waste collection and recycling.

---

## 3. Age Rating

* **Rating:** Ages 12 and older (K-12).
* When starting a new game, the game asks for your age. If the player is under 12, the game will stop.
